## You may, without asking

- Say that your project, device, tool or article works with Pitrig, is compatible with Pitrig, or
  runs Pitrig firmware, as long as the statement is true.
- Use the name to refer to this project in documentation, tutorials, videos, reviews, forum posts
  and shop listings for boards that the official firmware supports.
- Redistribute the official, unmodified builds under their own names.
- Use the name in the way any language needs it: to talk about the thing it names.

## You need written permission for

- Naming a **modified** firmware, configurator or fork Pitrig, or any name close enough to be

- Selling hardware, kits or assembled devices branded Pitrig, or presenting a product as official.
- Using the wordmark or the icon as the logo of your own product, company, service or channel, or in
  any way that suggests endorsement, affiliation or a partnership that does not exist.
- Registering domain names, package names, application-store entries or social accounts that contain
  Pitrig, or any trademark registration for the name.

## Modified builds

A device that ships a modified Pitrig firmware has to publish those modifications — that is the GPL,
and this policy does not touch it. What the policy asks in return is that the modified build carry
its own name, so that its bugs, its support and its reputation belong to whoever changed it rather
than to this project.

formality for anything that does not mislead people about who made what.
